# Vigil

## What it is

Vigil is a Chrome and Brave browser extension that pauses websites a person has chosen during a focus session. A companion site provides product information, account access, and subscription billing. Firefox is deferred.

## Why it exists

People often open a few familiar sites during time they meant to spend on work or study. Vigil tests whether a calm interruption can make it easier to return to the task without shaming people or removing their ability to choose.

## The problem it solves

Vigil gives someone a small, deliberate pause when they visit a selected site during a session. They can return to their task, wait until the session ends, or temporarily continue when they need access.

## How it reduces stress

The user chooses the sites and session length. The pause page says what happened, shows the remaining time, and provides a clear route back or a deliberate override. Rules and simple session counts stay on the device, and users can remove sites or clear the local data.

## What makes it distinct

Vigil keeps the decision with the person using it. It uses browser-native request rules rather than reading pages or building a browsing profile. The free focus loop works locally. Optional accounts use username/password sign-in and a one-time recovery code; email recovery, AI, and calendar connections are not required.

## Current limits

The extension only acts in a supported desktop browser and only on sites the user adds. Vigil does not guarantee that a user will avoid distractions or become more productive.
